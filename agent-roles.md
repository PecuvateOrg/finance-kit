# Agent roles

| Role | Does | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Finance agent** (your AI agent) | Finds facts, records and proves the books, drafts the accounts, runs dry runs, files after approval, keeps the proof | Files without "approved, file"; moves money; asks for a secret in chat; guesses a fact it could look up |
| **Director** (you) | Confirms the company, downloads what needs your login, explains what only you know, decides judgement calls, approves filings | Has to type figures into forms or reconcile anything |

There's one agent. Each stage's `CONTEXT.md` says which steps are the agent's and which are yours.
