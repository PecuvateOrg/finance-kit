# Companies House — package accounts upload (CICs)

checked: 2026-11-10 · fee: `prices.json` → `ch_cic_package`

**For:** filing a CIC's accounts and CIC34 together. CIC accounts can't go through ordinary filing software; the package ZIP (from `../filing/simplecompanytax.md`) is uploaded here.
**Help:** https://www.gov.uk/file-your-company-annual-accounts · CIC34 https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/form-cic34-community-interest-company-report
**Needs:** a Companies House online account linked to the company (the company authentication code; from the secret store, never chat), and card payment for the fee.
**Watch:** GOV.UK says that from 1 April 2028 accounts must be filed with commercial software. Check this route still exists before each filing.

**Done when:** Companies House emails acceptance, and the filed accounts show on the company's public page with the same figures as the draft. Save the acceptance email with the year's filing record.
