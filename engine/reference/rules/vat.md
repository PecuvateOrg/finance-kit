# VAT

checked: 2026-11-10 · review by: 2027-04-01 (or the next Budget) · figures: `vat.json` (the only copy)

## Registering
- **Must register** when taxable turnover for the **last 12 months** goes over the threshold (£90,000 from 1 April 2024; £85,000 before), or is **expected to in the next 30 days alone**. Check this rolling total at every month end, not just at the year end.
- Register within 30 days of the end of the month the threshold was passed; VAT is owed from the effective date HMRC sets, whether or not it was charged.
- **May cancel** when taxable turnover falls below £88,000. **May register voluntarily** below the threshold (often worth it when most customers are VAT-registered or costs carry a lot of VAT).
- Exempt sales (e.g. most financial and property income) don't count toward the threshold; zero-rated sales do. A non-profit's grants and donations are usually outside the scope of VAT: not counted.

## Rates
Standard 20% · reduced 5% · zero 0% · exempt (no VAT, and the VAT on related costs usually can't be reclaimed) · outside the scope. Every income and cost line gets one of these.

## Returns
- Usually quarterly. Filing **and** payment are due **1 month and 7 days** after the period ends.
- **Making Tax Digital applies to every VAT-registered business:** records kept digitally and the return sent by software that connects to HMRC. Typing figures into a website is not allowed.
- Box 1 VAT on sales · box 4 VAT reclaimed on purchases · box 6 sales excluding VAT · box 7 purchases excluding VAT. The return must equal the VAT control account in the books for the period.

## Schemes (the organisation chooses; the engine shows the difference)
- **Cash accounting:** VAT follows money received and paid, not invoice dates.
- **Flat rate scheme:** join if VAT turnover is up to £150,000. Pay a fixed % of gross turnover (set by business type) and reclaim no purchase VAT (except capital items over £2,000). A **limited cost business** (goods under 2% of turnover, or under £1,000 a year) pays 16.5%, which usually makes the scheme worse than standard.
- **Annual accounting:** one return a year with interim payments.

## For the books
VAT charged and reclaimed go to one VAT control account, never to income or costs. A business that isn't registered treats VAT paid as part of the cost.
