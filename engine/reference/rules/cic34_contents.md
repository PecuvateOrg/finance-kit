# CIC34 community interest company report — tool-neutral

Required every year from a CIC, delivered with its accounts (Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004 s34; Community Interest Company Regulations 2005 Part 7). Fee £15. Source: [GOV.UK: form CIC34](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/form-cic34-community-interest-company-report) (forms updated 29 January 2025). Checked 2026-11-10.

**Which version:** most CICs use the **simplified** report. Use the **detailed** report if the CIC paid performance-related interest on a debt or debenture, or (CIC limited by shares) declared or proposes dividends for the year.

**Header:** company name exactly as on the accounts, company number, year ending written in full (e.g. "31 October 2026") matching the accounts. Dates must be written in full throughout or the report is rejected.

| Part | What it must say | If nothing applies, write exactly | Engine source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Activities and impact | What the company did in the year and how the community benefited | — (always required) | Activity records; the director's account; figures with their source |
| 2. Consultation with stakeholders | Who the stakeholders are, how they were consulted, what was done in response | "There has been no stakeholder consultation held" | Director input, feedback records |
| 3. Directors' remuneration | Remuneration received by directors, or where the accounts give full details | "no remuneration was received by the directors" | Payroll and the accounts; must equal the accounts |
| 4. Transfers of assets other than for full consideration | Each transfer to an asset-locked body or for the community, with the amount or a fair estimate | "no transfer of assets other than for full consideration has been made" | Fixed asset register; director confirms |
| 5. Signatory | Signed and dated by a director | — | Director |

A social audit report covering Parts 1 and 2 may be attached instead of writing them. The engine drafts Parts 1–4 from sourced figures, marks anything unsourced for the director, and cross-checks Part 3 against payroll and the accounts.
