# Confirmation statement — tool-neutral

Every company files one at least once every 12 months, even if nothing has changed. Source: [GOV.UK: confirmation statement guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/confirmation-statement-guidance), [file online](https://www.gov.uk/file-your-confirmation-statement-with-companies-house). Checked 2026-11-10; review by 2027-04-06.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| When | Up to 14 days after the end of the review period (12 months from incorporation or the last statement's date). Can be filed early, which restarts the review period |
| Fee | £50 online; £110 on paper (CS01) |
| Not filing | Fine up to £5,000 and the company may be struck off |
| Identity verification | Before filing, every director must have verified their identity with Companies House (GOV.UK One Login) and the filing needs each director's **personal code** |
| Registered email address | Must be given if not already on record |
| Lawful purpose | The company confirms its intended future activities are lawful |
| Confirmed as up to date | Directors and secretary, people with significant control (PSCs), registered office, registered email |
| Updated in the statement itself if changed | SIC codes, statement of capital (companies with shares), shareholders, trading status of shares, PSC exemption |

**What the engine prepares:** compares Companies House's public record with what the director says has changed, lists each answer to give, reminds each director to have their personal code ready, and records the date filed. Changes to officers, PSCs or the registered office are filed as separate updates before the statement, never only inside it.
