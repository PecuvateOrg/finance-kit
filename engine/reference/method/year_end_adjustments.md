# Year-end adjustments

The bank shows when money moved; the accounts show what belongs to the year. These adjustments close the gap. Each is decided by the director (or the agent, where marked) and recorded with who decided it.

- **Opening position = last year's filed closing position.** Never assume opening balances or work them out as a balancing figure: every figure in the accounts depends on them. First year of trading: nil apart from share capital.
- **Check last year against the books.** A cost last year already counted as owed isn't this year's cost when it's paid. Something last year left out (for example card debt) is corrected through reserves, without refiling. A correction through reserves may still change the tax: work out its tax effect and tell the director before filing.
- **Accruals:** costs that belong to the year but are paid after it (for example the accounts or filing fee). When last year's accrual is paid at a slightly different amount, release the difference to the same cost account (the agent may do this and tells the director).
- **Prepayments and deferred income:** the reverse — paid or received this year for next year (a club paid in advance is income of the year the sessions run).
- **Closing stock:** what's on hand at the year end reduces this year's costs. Nil if goods are only sent after sale.
- **Depreciation and capital allowances:** a fixed asset is spread over its life in the accounts; tax uses capital allowances instead. Not yet covered by this engine.
- **Flat-rate claims to the director:** working from home and business mileage, at HMRC's rates (`../rules/expense_rates.md`), owed to the director unless already paid.
- **Going concern:** if liabilities exceed assets, say what signing the accounts confirms (usually that the director won't demand their loan back ahead of other creditors).
- **The tax charge:** the year's corporation tax is a cost of the year and a liability until paid (`../rules/corporation_tax.md`).
